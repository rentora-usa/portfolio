Level 1 — Quarter 1

Five stages. Every one of them is a category on the rubric, so clearing all five means the quarter is finished and documented.

Stage 1 60 pts (shared)

Formal writing

Honors track: six extra essays this quarter, each with the essay itself and the thinking that produced it. Due dates come from Google Classroom, and late work is not accepted.

Boss requirements

  • Six essays, submitted on time
  • Essay format followed
  • “Thinking on paper” included with each — outlines, scratch notes, cut paragraphs
  • Depth over length: an argument the reader has to sit with

Stage 2 Bonus only

Informal writing

This stage only adds points, never subtracts them. Anything you started and abandoned goes here: essays that fizzled out, half-arguments, ideas that went nowhere but took real thinking to reach.

What counts

  • Abandoned drafts, kept honestly as drafts
  • A line or two on why each one stalled
  • Free writes, rants, fragments, openings without endings
